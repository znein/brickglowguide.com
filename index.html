Questions people ask

Do I need to take my set apart?

No. BRKOX kits are designed to fit an already-assembled model – you may need to temporarily remove a few bricks to route lights and cables, and set-specific instructions show where each light goes.

Does the kit include the LEGO® set?

No. These are lighting accessories for sets you already own (or are about to buy). LEGO® is a trademark of the LEGO Group, which does not sponsor or endorse these products or this site.

How are they powered?

The kits we've checked are USB powered. Check the product page for the exact power option on the kit you're looking at.

Is shipping free?

BRKOX offers free standard shipping on US orders (typically 1–3 days to process, 7–15 business days in transit). Final shipping costs are shown at their checkout.
